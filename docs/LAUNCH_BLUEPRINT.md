> This is the authoritative launch plan. Anchor date: **Target Beta Launch: 5 September 2026**.

**Objective:** Finalize product, compliance, operations, and go-to-market during August.

---

## 1. Product

Finalize Android, iOS, Web, QA, bug fixes, analytics, crash monitoring.

## 2. Compliance

GST, DLT, Privacy Policy, Terms, Refund Policy, Trademark planning.

## 3. Infrastructure

Supabase, domains, backups, monitoring, production environment.

## 4. Payments

Razorpay onboarding, webhook testing, settlements.

## 5. Identity & Trust

KYC APIs, moderation, fraud prevention.

## 6. Communication

Email, SMS, WhatsApp templates, notifications.

## 7. Marketing

Landing page, social media, launch campaign, referral program.

## 8. Operations

Vendor onboarding SOP, customer support SOP, escalation matrix.

## 9. Finance

Grant utilization, monthly budget, accounting.

## 10. KPIs

Users, vendors, orders, activation, retention, NPS.

## 11. Risks

Technical, legal, adoption, financial and mitigation.

- **Week 3:** Closed beta with vendors/users, fix critical issues.
