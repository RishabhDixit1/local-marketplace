# ServiQ Product Overview

## What Is ServiQ?

ServiQ is a hyperlocal services marketplace connecting consumers who need home/services with verified local providers in Delhi NCR. Think of it as an "Urban Company for your neighborhood."

## Target Market

- **Geography:** Delhi NCR (currently: Crossing Republik, Shahberi, Gaur City, Greater Noida West)
- **Users:** Residents of housing societies and local markets
- **Providers:** Local service professionals (electricians, plumbers, AC repair, cleaning, carpenters, painters, etc.)

## Core Value Proposition

**For Consumers:**
- AI-powered search: "I need an electrician near me" → matched with verified local providers
- Compare quotes from multiple providers
- Book and pay through the app
- Track service status in real-time
- Read reviews and trust scores

**For Providers:**
- Get discovered by nearby customers
- Manage bookings, quotes, and payments
- Build reputation through reviews
- Grow with subscriptions and featured placements
- Team/workspace management for businesses

## Platform Surfaces

1. **Web App** (https://www.serviqapp.com) - Full-featured marketplace
2. **Mobile App** (Android APK, iOS compile-ready) - Native experience
3. **Public Landing** - SEO-optimized landing pages for localities

## Revenue Model

| Stream | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Subscriptions | Essential (₹299/mo), Premium (₹999/mo) |
| Commission | Per-transaction commission on completed orders |
| Featured Placements | Providers pay for boosted visibility |
| Promotions | Promo codes and campaigns |
